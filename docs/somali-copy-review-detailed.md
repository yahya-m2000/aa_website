# Somali copy review — Somaliland audience

This is an editorial first pass for the public website, not a certified Isaaq-dialect translation. It targets clear Somaliland-facing shopping copy. Your dad's preferred wording is the final reference. Other Somali varieties are not being classified as incorrect.

## What changed

- Kept the approved slogan **Hadda Ka Iibso** in the hero and download sections.
- Replaced literal English marketing phrases with direct descriptions of shopping, searching, selecting products, and getting help.
- Distinguished **dalab** (an order) from **qiimo-dhimis** (a discount). The latter is the proposed wording for the app's Deals section.
- Made **xidhiidh**, **telefoon**, and **iimayl** consistent in the active navigation and form.
- Introduced Somaliland in the hero's audience context without claiming that the service is exclusive to Somaliland.
- Corrected the APK instruction that previously said **dalool**, and preserved Android's recognizable setting label.
- Kept the email notice explicit: the button prepares a draft; the visitor sends it from their email app.

The existing testimonial has a grammatical problem: **“dukaanka timaha ee ay ku riyoonayey.”** I have left attributed quotations and video captions unchanged pending comparison with the speaker's actual words. Please ask Dad to check that quotation against the video, particularly the intended description of the business. Customer names, locations, and numeric claims were not rewritten.

## Evidence and limits

[Somaliland's presidential contact page](https://madaxtooyadajsl.com/xidhiidh/) uses **Xidhiidh**, supporting that spelling choice. This is evidence of local written usage, not proof that every proposed sentence is idiomatic Isaaq speech.

[Dahabshiil's Somali contact page](https://www.dahabshiil.com/so/contact/) provides commercial-language context. Its wording is not treated as a dialect authority or copied as a translation standard.

The pass covers copy used by the current home/download pages and their shared navigation, footer, form, categories, and labels. Older unused marketing strings remain in the translation file; they should be reviewed if those components are restored. The English copy, admin portal, routing language code, and launch slogan are unchanged.
